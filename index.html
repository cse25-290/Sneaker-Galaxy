Sneaker Brand History

Nike

Founded: 1964

Founders: Phil Knight & Bill Bowerman

Popular Sneakers: Air Force 1, Air Max, Air Jordan

About: Nike is one of the biggest sportswear brands in the world known for innovation and athlete collaborations.

Adidas

Founded: 1949

Founder: Adolf Dassler

Popular Sneakers: Superstar, Stan Smith, Yeezy

About: Adidas is famous for its three-stripe design and combines sportswear with fashion culture.

Puma

Founded: 1948

Founder: Rudolf Dassler

Popular Sneakers: Suede Classic, RS-X

About: Puma is known for stylish sportswear and collaborations with athletes and celebrities.

New Balance

Founded: 1906

Founder: William J. Riley

Popular Sneakers: 990 Series, 574

About: New Balance is respected for comfort, quality craftsmanship, and running shoes.

Jordan

Founded: 1984

Founder: Nike with Michael Jordan

Popular Sneakers: Air Jordan 1, Air Jordan 4, Air Jordan 11

About: Jordan Brand became one of the most iconic sneaker lines in basketball and streetwear culture.
